# Provider artwork

These are original provider marks, not generic action icons. They are served locally and shared by the marketing homepage, connection settings, Spotify UI, and live workspace. Brand artwork retains its original colors; the homepage’s color variants never recolor it. Marks identify integrations and do not imply endorsement.
